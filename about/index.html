About

About confined-space.com

confined-space.com is a dedicated confined space training resource connected to Industrial Certified Training, LLC. Training courses are provided through HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC, which delivers OSHA, EPA, DOT, hazardous materials, and workplace safety training across online, SCORM, virtual instructor-led, and in-person formats.

What This Site Is

A Focused Landing Site for Confined Space Training

confined-space.com exists to give employers, supervisors, and workers a clear, role-specific path into confined space training: Awareness, Permit-Required Entry, Supervisor, and Competent Person, without wading through a full safety-training catalog to find them.

🏢

Relationship to Industrial Certified Training, LLC

confined-space.com is a subsidiary brand of Industrial Certified Training, LLC, a company specializing in OSHA, EPA, and IACET-accredited training.

🤝

Relationship to HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC

Confined space courses on this site are provided and delivered through HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC, an IACET-accredited safety education provider. HAZWOPER OSHA Training issues the certificates of completion and hosts the underlying course platform.

Who Provides the Training

Course content is built and taught by HAZWOPER OSHA Training's instructional team. See our Instructors & Training Provider page.

Why This Site Exists

Why confined-space.com

Confined space incidents remain a leading cause of multiple-fatality events in construction and general industry. Every role on a confined space job, from the person just working nearby to the one designing the rescue plan, needs training scoped to their actual responsibilities. This site was built to make that role-by-role path easy to find, with honest language about what a certificate does and doesn't mean.
