Permit-Required Entry vs. Supervisor Training

Entry training is for the people who go inside or watch the door. Supervisor training is for the person who authorizes the permit in the first place.

3 Hours · $59.99

Permit-Required Entry

For entrants and attendants who execute the entry itself: testing the atmosphere, completing the permit paperwork, monitoring conditions, and following non-entry rescue procedures.

  • Entrant and attendant duties
  • Atmospheric testing and ventilation
  • Does not include permit authorization authority
Course Details
VS
5 Hours · $69.99

Confined Space Supervisor

For entry supervisors who authorize and cancel entry permits, verify conditions before entry, and take overall responsibility for the entry operation.

  • Permit authorization & entry approval
  • Equipment and monitoring verification
  • Emergency response coordination authority
Course Details

The deciding question: does this person sign off on the entry permit and take responsibility for the entry, or execute the entry once it's authorized? Signing authority requires Supervisor training. Many smaller crews train the same person in both roles.
