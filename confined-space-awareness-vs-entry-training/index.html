Awareness vs. Permit-Required Entry

The most common mix-up: Awareness is for people who work near confined spaces. Entry training is for the people who actually go inside.

3 Hours · $49.99

Confined Space Awareness

Foundational hazard recognition for anyone who works near or around confined spaces without entering them. Covers definitions, hazard types, and basic safe work practices.

  • Does not authorize entry into a permit space
  • No atmospheric testing or permit procedures
  • Good for general crews, site visitors, new hires
Course Details
VS
3 Hours · $59.99

Permit-Required Entry

For entrants who physically enter permit-required confined spaces, and attendants who monitor them from outside. Covers the full entry permit system.

  • Covers completing and interpreting an entry permit
  • Atmospheric testing order and ventilation checks
  • Non-entry rescue and evacuation procedures
Course Details

The deciding question: will this person physically enter a permit-required confined space, or monitor someone who does? If yes, Awareness training alone is not enough. They need Permit-Required Entry training. If they only work nearby without entering, Awareness training is the appropriate starting point.
