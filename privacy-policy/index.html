Privacy Policy

How your information is handled when you use confined-space.com and enroll in a course.

confined-space.com is a subsidiary brand of Industrial Certified Training, LLC. Course enrollment, payment processing, student records, and account data are handled through HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC's training platform, the provider that delivers and administers these courses.

Because enrollment, certificates, and student records live on HAZWOPER OSHA Training's systems, their privacy policy governs how that data is collected, used, and protected. confined-space.com does not maintain a separate data-handling system from HAZWOPER OSHA Training's platform.
