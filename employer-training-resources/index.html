Employer Training Resources

Free, printable templates to help you document the site-specific and hands-on steps that OSHA-aligned online training doesn't cover by itself: recordkeeping, site-specific addenda, hands-on competency, and rescue-plan verification. Open any section below and use your browser's Print (or Save as PDF) function.

These are general-purpose templates provided for convenience, not a legal compliance guarantee. Adapt every field to your actual site, hazards, and written confined space program, and have your safety manager or counsel review your final program. Templates are provided as-is; completing them does not substitute for employer-required training, evaluation, or OSHA compliance obligations. See our Credential Transparency page for what a course certificate does and doesn't cover.

3. Hands-On Competency Checklist

Use after online coursework to confirm the worker can actually perform the equipment tasks their role requires, not just answer questions about them.

  • Dons and adjusts full-body harness correctly without assistance
  • Operates gas monitor: power-on, bump test, and reads display correctly
  • Demonstrates correct order of atmospheric testing (O2, LEL, toxics)
  • Operates ventilation equipment used on this site
  • Demonstrates correct entry permit completion
  • Demonstrates non-entry retrieval system operation
  • Demonstrates two-way communication procedure with attendant/supervisor
  • States correct emergency/evacuation procedure from memory

5. PPE & Retrieval-Equipment Signoff Form

Confirms required PPE and retrieval equipment were inspected and are in service before entry.

  • Full-body harness inspected, no cuts/fraying/damage
  • Retrieval line / winch inspected and rated for the entrant's weight plus equipment
  • Calibrated multi-gas monitor present and functioning
  • Respiratory protection matches the hazard assessment for this space
  • Communication equipment tested and functioning

6. Rescue-Plan Verification Checklist

Per 1926.1211, rescue capability must be verified and ready before entry begins. See our full rescue planning guide for more detail.

  • Rescue method identified for this space (non-entry retrieval vs. entry rescue)
  • Rescue provider identified by name (in-house team or contracted service)
  • Rescue provider has confirmed it can respond to this specific space in time
  • If relying on public emergency services, they've been contacted in advance and confirmed capability
  • Rescue equipment on-site, inspected, and matched to this space's configuration
  • Attendant knows how to summon rescue without entering the space

7. Retraining Trigger Checklist

OSHA doesn't set one universal refresher interval; retraining is required whenever any of the following occur. Use this log to track triggers as they happen.

  • Job duties changed for the affected employee
  • A new hazard was introduced to the confined space or the work
  • An evaluation or incident showed training wasn't effective
  • Equipment, procedures, or the permit system changed
  • 36 months have elapsed since the last certificate (recommended interval)

8. Role-Specific Pre-Entry Checklists

Quick-reference checklists for each role to review immediately before entry. Not a substitute for full course training, listed here for on-site convenience.

Entrant

  • Reviewed the entry permit and understands the hazards present
  • Wearing required PPE and retrieval equipment correctly
  • Knows the communication signal/schedule with the attendant
  • Knows to exit immediately if ordered or if warning signs appear

Attendant

  • Remains outside the space for the entire entry, no other duties
  • Maintains continuous communication with the entrant(s)
  • Monitors for unauthorized entry by others
  • Knows how to summon rescue without entering the space

Entry Supervisor

  • Verified atmospheric testing and acceptable entry conditions
  • Confirmed all entrants/attendants are trained for their roles
  • Confirmed rescue capability is verified and ready
  • Signed and authorized the entry permit; will cancel it if conditions change

Competent Person

  • Confirmed the space has been correctly identified and classified
  • Confirmed the written permit program is current for this site
  • Confirmed rescue and emergency arrangements are documented
  • Reviewed recordkeeping for all personnel assigned to this entry
